Curated Knowledge

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I utilize the Smart Room controls?

Once you arrive at your selected property, your dashboard automatically unlocks the "Smart Room" feature. This allows you to control lighting, climate, and entertainment systems directly from your smartphone via our secure IoT network.

What is 360° Room Exploration?

LuxeStay offers a high-definition digital tour of your specific suite tier. This immersive experience allows you to virtually step into your room before you arrive, helping you get familiar with the layout and premium amenities available.

How does Express Checkout work?

You can skip the reception desk entirely. On your departure day, simply select "Express Checkout" from your dashboard to finalize your bill, leave feedback, and receive your digital invoice instantly via email.

Can I request elite services like Fine Dining or Housekeeping?

Yes, the "Guest Services" panel on your dashboard allows you to order from our gourmet menu or request housekeeping with a single tap. All requests are tracked in real-time by our on-site platinum concierge team.

Is my data and booking history kept secure?

Absolutely. LuxeStay utilizes enterprise-grade encryption and secure authentication through Supabase to protect your personal details, financial records, and booking history at all times.
